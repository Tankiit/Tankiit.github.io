I work on when a learning system's uncertainty can be trusted: which structural conditions — named concepts, compiled symbolic frames, decision procedures — must hold before an uncertainty report means anything, and how to build models that satisfy them.
{: .lede}

Before CRIL I was a postdoctoral researcher at VUB (Brussels), working on medical imaging and out-of-distribution detection. I hold a PhD in multimodal learning from Queen Mary University of London.
