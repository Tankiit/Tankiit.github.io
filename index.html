Research

One question organises my work: what structure must a learning system be given before the uncertainty it reports about its own output becomes identifiable? Most work on uncertainty asks how to estimate it. I ask the prior question of whether the estimate refers to anything — whether there is a well-defined quantity behind the number labelled "epistemic", or only an artefact of how the model happened to be trained. My answer is that symbolic structure — named concept layers, compiled logical frames, explicit decision procedures — supplies the referent that a learned representation lacks, and that this hybridisation is what makes an uncertainty claim well-posed rather than merely plausible.

That question splits into four, and across them uncertainty changes role: first a nuisance to be averaged away, then content to be preserved, then a representation to be constructed, and finally a measurement to be justified.

  • When is a set-valued uncertainty functional identified? Credal sets and belief functions promise to separate reducible ignorance from irreducible noise. Under what conditions on data, supervision, and architecture is that separation unique, and when does it collapse into a relabelling of the point estimate?
  • Which frames admit certifiable evaluation? Set-valued models are usually built with disjoint focal sets for tractability, and under disjointness their width reduces to a reparameterisation of the point prediction. Restoring non-degeneracy needs mass over a subset lattice — intractable by enumeration, tractable exactly when the representation admits a compiled form. What does knowledge compilation buy for uncertainty, and what can be certified about it?
  • Can the concept layer be learned rather than stipulated? Concept bottleneck models assume the concepts are given. I want discovery procedures that produce concepts a human can name and audit, without circularity between what is discovered and what is used to evaluate it.
  • Which decisions does the measurement license? Expert deferral, span-level flagging in generated text, group-wise comparison. For each, the evidence that licenses acting on an uncertainty report should itself be observable, so that the instrument can be audited and not just trusted.

I work on these questions in language models and vision-language models, with medical imaging as the application that keeps the stakes honest.
